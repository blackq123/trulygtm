This call is for you if

You're a B2B SaaS operator with a real GTM problem.

  • You're a founder, head of growth, head of marketing, or VP sales
  • Your company is between $1M and $50M ARR

Quick FAQ

Before you book.

Who's actually on the call?
Akash, the founder. Not an SDR, not a sales rep, not an account manager. The person doing the work is the person on the call.
Will you try to sell me on the call?
No. We'll tell you if we can help and roughly what it would cost. If you want to move forward we send a scoping doc afterwards. No pitch deck, no urgency tactics.
What if I just want quick advice?
Totally fine. Plenty of calls end with us giving 2–3 actionable suggestions and the person not hiring us. We treat the 30 mins as useful regardless.
Can I bring my team?
Yes — up to 3 people from your side. More than that and the call becomes a meeting, which doesn't help anyone.
