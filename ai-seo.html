FAQ

AI SEO, demystified.

What's the difference between AEO, GEO, and LLM SEO?
They're synonyms with marketing teams arguing about which one wins. AEO = Answer Engine Optimization (older term, more focused on featured snippets + AI). GEO = Generative Engine Optimization (newer term, broader). LLM SEO = literally optimizing for large language models. We use "AI SEO" as the umbrella because that's what buyers search for. The work is the same.
How long until I see citations?
For most B2B SaaS clients, first new citations appear in 30–45 days. Meaningful share-of-voice movement takes 60–90 days. AI-attributed traffic usually shows up in GA4 around week 8. Anyone promising "results in 30 days" is selling you vibes.
Do I still need traditional SEO?
Yes, and we do both. Most LLMs still draw heavily from Google's index, so ranking well organically is a leading indicator of getting cited. We treat it as one engagement, not two.
How do you actually track AI visibility?
We run a custom prompt set tailored to your buyer journey across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Claude, weekly. We track citation share, sentiment, position in answer, and competitor co-mentions. You see the dashboard. We don't keep it behind a paywall.
