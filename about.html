About trulygtm

14 years of building what agencies only talk about.

trulygtm is not an agency with a bench of freelancers. It is a focused GTM practice built by operators who have spent their careers inside B2B SaaS companies. We don't advise from the outside. We have done this work ourselves, at scale, with real pipeline numbers attached.

What we bring to the table

Our team has 14+ years of combined experience across B2B SaaS marketing, product marketing, content strategy, SEO, cold outreach, and video marketing. We have worked inside product companies, not just for them. That means we understand onboarding flows, competitive positioning, feature launches, and the full buyer journey from first search to closed deal.

We built and scaled a 20-person marketing team at one of the fastest-growing cold outreach platforms in the market. We grew organic traffic from scratch to over 1M monthly visits. We launched products, managed YouTube channels, owned deliverability playbooks, and ran GTM for multiple product lines.

When AI started changing how buyers discover software, we didn't wait. We started building AEO, GEO, and LLM SEO playbooks in early 2024 and presented them on stage at ADMM (Ahmedabad Digital Marketing Meetup). These frameworks are now part of every engagement we run.
