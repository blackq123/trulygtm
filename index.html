How is "AI SEO" different from regular SEO?
Regular SEO ranks pages on Google. AI SEO (also called AEO, GEO, or LLM SEO) gets your brand cited inside ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews. The work overlaps but the deliverables are different — schema, citation building, entity optimization, and structured answer content.
Do you do SEO and content together, or separately?
Together. Splitting them is what slow agencies do. Our content writers and SEO leads sit in the same Slack channel and ship under the same brief. You get one team, one number to beat.
What kind of companies do you work with?
B2B SaaS, mostly Series A through C, $1M – $50M ARR. We've done sales tech, marketing tech, AI tools, and dev tools. If your buyer searches Google or ChatGPT before they buy, we can help.
What does this cost?
Single-channel retainers start at $4K/month. Multi-channel engagements typically run $8–15K/month. We don't quote without an audit — pricing depends on what's actually broken.
Do you lock people into long contracts?
No. Month-to-month with 30-day notice. If we're not earning the retainer, you should fire us. Our entire pricing model is built around that assumption.
