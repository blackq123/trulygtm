FAQ

Common questions.

Do you use AI to write?
For research, outlining, and SERP analysis — yes. For the actual prose — no. Every word that ships is written by a human writer who understands B2B SaaS. We're happy to show you the writing process on a sample piece before you commit.
How many pieces per month?
Most retainers are 4, 8, or 12 pieces/month — mixed across articles, comparison pages, and landing pages. We can also do programmatic SEO sprints (100+ pages from a template in one cycle).
Will the writers actually understand my product?
Yes. Every writer on the team has shipped 100+ articles in B2B SaaS specifically. We do a 60-minute product onboarding before the first piece, and we sit on sales calls regularly. If a piece sounds generic, we rewrite it on us.
What does it cost?
Per-piece pricing starts at $400 for short articles and goes to $1,200+ for deep research pieces or comparison pages. Most clients run a $4–8K/month retainer for 8–12 pieces with strategy and reporting.
Do you handle SEO too, or just writing?
Both. Our writers and SEO leads work as one team. Most clients bundle this with our AI SEO retainer for compounding results.
