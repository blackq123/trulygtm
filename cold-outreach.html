FAQ

Common questions.

Will my main domain get burned?
No. We never send cold from your primary domain. We buy and warm 2–4 secondary domains (e.g. tryyourbrand.com, getyourbrand.io) and route all cold sending through them. Your main domain stays clean for marketing and transactional email.
Do you write the copy or do I?
We write it. We sit on a 60-min call with you (or your founder/CRO) to capture how you actually talk about the product, then we draft. You approve before anything sends. Ongoing copy refreshes are included.
What's your reply rate benchmark?
8–14% for B2B SaaS targeting mid-market. Higher for ICP-narrow campaigns; lower for broad SMB. Anyone promising 20%+ is either lying or counting auto-replies.
Do you do LinkedIn outreach too?
Yes — and we run cold email + LinkedIn as one orchestrated sequence. Email step 1, LinkedIn connect step 2, email step 3, LinkedIn DM step 4. Multi-channel always outperforms single-channel.
How much does this cost?
Retainers start at $4K/month for a single-mailbox-rotation campaign. Multi-channel cold + LinkedIn typically runs $6–10K/month depending on send volume and lead sourcing scope. Tooling and domain costs are passed through at cost.
